# Business Requirements Document

AI Digital Studio — brief-to-media generation

## Purpose

The team requires an **AI Digital Studio**: a solution that accepts a **creative brief** and automatically produces finished **media deliverables** — **video, image, audio, podcast, and PowerPoint** — to support the design, prototyping, evaluation, and commercialisation of next-generation research, intelligence, and decision-support products.

The core solution requirement is therefore simple to state and is the frame for everything that follows:

> **Brief in → media out.** A user submits a brief (objective, audience, source research, brand, deliverable type); the Studio plans, generates, assembles, reviews, and delivers the requested media asset.

The internal consumer products (including a new, under-development consumer research platform and a category-management suite) require presentation and user-experience outputs that extend beyond traditional reports, dashboards, and presentations. The Studio produces those premium multimedia deliverables, and can also enrich them with agentic AI search and secure browser navigation to retrieve external supporting evidence.

Whilst cost control is important, it should be noted that the Studio will primarily be used by the consumer research platform, which will operate on a high-margin commercial model, allowing investment in premium generation providers to attract and retain customers, as well as creating differentiation from conventional competitor offerings.

The generation abilities the Studio depends on (voice, video, image, avatars, agentic research, presentations) may be delivered by internal or external providers. As a supporting concern, the architecture function is therefore asked to determine whether these underlying capabilities already exist within the organisation, whether existing capabilities can be extended, or whether external solutions should be integrated behind the Studio — without changing the Studio's brief-to-media contract.

The requested capabilities should enable the organisation to:

## Universal Technical Requirements

All proposed solutions should satisfy the following minimum requirements.

### API Accessibility

- Solutions must expose comprehensive, production-grade, well-documented APIs.
- API functionality should provide access to substantially the same controls and capability available via the user interface.
- Preference should be given to solutions where advanced controls are fully accessible programmatically and are not restricted to manual user interaction only.

### Integration Capability

Solutions should support:

- Enterprise integration patterns.
- Programmatic access.
- Job queuing.
- Callbacks upon completion.

### n8n Compatibility

Preference should be given to solutions that:

- Provide native n8n nodes as preference, otherwise reliable and secure community nodes if possible.
- Provide officially supported integrations.
- Provide mature and actively maintained community nodes.

### Scalability

Solutions should be suitable for:

- Individual experimentation.
- Team-level use.
- Product integration.
- Multiple concurrent programmatic connections.
- Future enterprise-scale deployment.

#### Cost control

Solutions should, ideally, offer:

- Billing and consumption reports.
- Spending limits.
- Pay-per-use entry packages without subscriptions or minimum commitments.
- Potential for tiered rate reduction or negotiation.
